# The HMAP Framework (v2.5)

The **Human-Mediated Agentic Process (HMAP)** is the governing philosophy of Project Hypatia. It defines the interface between human intuition and machine intelligence.

## The Problem: The "Black Box"
Standard AI tools often provide single-shot answers (e.g., "Write me a paper on Mars"). This approach lacks transparency, rigor, and human alignment, leading to hallucination-prone results.

## The HMAP Solution
HMAP breaks the research process into a **Chain of Mediated Tokens**.

### Pillar 1: Human Sovereignty
The human (the Principal Investigator) is the **Ultimate Arbitrator**. 
- The AI cannot "advance" the project state autonomously without a verification signal. 
- The human must click "Verify Node" after every step, ensuring they have reviewed and approved the logic before it becomes "Context" for the next milestone.

### Pillar 2: Agentic Execution
While the human directs, the AI **Autonomously Executes**. 
- In the "Self-Healing Debugger," the human doesn't need to fix code. 
- The agent identifies its own failures, analyzes errors, and iterates until the human's goal is met.

### Pillar 3: Contextual Reciprocity
As the human edits an output, the AI's "Reality" changes. 
- If you edit Step 3's hypothesis to be more specific, Step 4's methodology will automatically adapt to that new manual input. 
- This creates a **Feedback Loop** where the AI learns from your scientific intuition.

## HMAP Interaction Pattern
1. **Human Prompt**: Defines the initial constraints.
2. **AI Synthesis**: Drafts a proposed archival node.
3. **Human Mediation**: Edits, refines, or regenerates the node.
4. **AI Adaptation**: Uses the approved node as "Ground Truth" for the next logical step.

## Why HMAP?
In science, the **Process** is as important as the **Result**. HMAP preserves the "Scientific Audit Trail," allowing you to justify every decision in your final paper.
