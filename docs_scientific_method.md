# Scientific Rigor & Theoretical Framework

Project Hypatia is a digital formalization of the **Empirical Scientific Method**. This document outlines the logical rigor applied to each discovery node within the platform.

## The Logical Chain of Inference
Each step in Hypatia serves a specific epistemic purpose in the construction of knowledge:

1. **Refinement (Step 1)**: Moving from raw observation to a **Falsifiable Question**.
2. **Grounding (Step 2)**: Situating the question within the **Global Body of Knowledge** via search grounding.
3. **Prediction (Step 3)**: Proposing specific, measurable outcomes ($H_1, H_2, H_0$).
4. **Operationalization (Step 4 & 5)**: Defining the exact **Variables** and **Measurement Protocols**.
5. **Observation (Step 6)**: Gathering empirical data through **Simulation** or **In-Situ Measurement**.
6. **Inference (Step 7 & 8)**: Applying **Statistical Interpretation** to determine if results support the hypothesis.
7. **Adversarial Testing (Step 9)**: Subjecting the logic to **Skeptical Peer Critique**.
8. **Dissemination (Step 10)**: Formal synthesis and communication of findings.

## Falsifiability
The core of Hypatia is the ability to **Fail**. 
- In Step 8, the AI is programmed to identify if the generated data *refutes* your hypothesis. 
- A "Failed" hypothesis is a **Successful Scientific Result**, as it eliminates a false path in the search space of truth.

## Agentic Neutrality
The AI agents are prompted to maintain **Methodological Objectivity**. 
- The **Peer Reviewer (Step 9)** is specifically instructed to find flaws, even in "perfect-sounding" research. 
- Do not ignore these critiques; they are the "Skeptical Guardrails" that prevent AI hallucination and logical drift.

## Theoretical vs. Empirical Boundaries
### Theoretical Modeling
Hypatia excels at **Theoretical Modeling**. If you are performing physical laboratory work, use Hypatia to design the methodology and analyze results, but do not rely on Step 6's "Synthesis" for real-world chemical or biological confirmation.

### Synthetic Data Bias
Synthetic data generated in Step 6 follows the **Methodological Assumptions** of Step 4. It is excellent for verifying that a methodology *could* yield results, but it cannot discover "Black Swan" events that have not been theorized or modeled in the initial variables.
