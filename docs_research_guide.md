# Strategic Research Guide (Advanced)

Conducting high-fidelity research with AI requires transitioning from a "user" to a **Principal Investigator (PI)**. This guide outlines the strategies used to maximize the scientific rigor of your Hypatia projects.

## 1. Defining the Research Frontier
When starting a project in Step 1, avoid broad, generic topics. Target the **frontier**:
- **Specific Interaction**: Instead of "Caffeine effects," use "The synergistic effect of caffeine and L-Theanine on hippocampal neuroplasticity in sleep-deprived murine models."
- **Novel Context**: Instead of "Solar energy," use "Efficiency decay of perovskite solar cells under high-humidity tropical conditions."

> **The Novelty Pulse**: In Step 1, if your **Uniqueness Score** is below 0.6, the AI is warning you that the topic is saturated. Try adding a unique constraint or a new variable to increase scientific value.

## 2. Advanced Prompting & Archival Feedback
If an AI output in any step is too generic, do not just accept it. Use the **Archival Feedback** loop:
- **Be Specific**: "Incorporate more focus on metabolic pathways rather than just behavioral results."
- **Statistical Rigor**: "Ensure the methodology includes a double-blind protocol and a sample size power analysis."
- **Constraint Addition**: "Limit the scope of the literature review to papers published after 2020."

## 3. The "Self-Healing" Workflow
In Step 6 (Experiment Runner), the AI Debugger can resolve syntax errors, but it cannot solve **conceptual errors**. 
- If the simulation code keeps failing, check your **Methodology (Step 4)**. 
- Ensure your methodology defines clear, quantifiable variables ($x, y, z$) that can be translated into a mathematical model.

## 4. Verification & Validation
AI is an **Agent**, not an **Oracle**. Use this verification checklist:
- **Literature (Step 2)**: Verify that the journals cited actually exist. AI search grounding is powerful but not infallible.
- **Data (Step 7)**: Check if the charts reflect the raw CSV data. Download the CSV and verify the trends manually for critical nodes.
- **Publication (Step 10)**: Read the Discussion section carefully. Does it logically connect the findings to the initial hypothesis without "leapfrogging" the data?

## 5. Ethical Considerations
As the PI, you are responsible for:
- **Bias Identification**: Be critical of findings that seem too "perfect." 
- **Safety Protocol**: Never use Hypatia to simulate dangerous biological, chemical, or weaponized protocols.
- **Transparency**: Always disclose that Project Hypatia was used as a research assistant in your final academic acknowledgments.
